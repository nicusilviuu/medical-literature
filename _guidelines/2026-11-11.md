## No new guidelines from tracked societies today

**No new guidelines from tracked societies today.** SFAR, SPILF, SRLF first, then ESAIC, ESC, ESPEN, ESICM,
SCCM, IACTS, EACTS, STS and ASA. **The society route returned only documents from 2012 to 2025, all of them
either already held here or outside scope** — an SRLF/SFAR/SFMU intrahospital transport document from 2012, the
SFAR/SRLF intubation and extubation guideline, the 2021 fluid-choice guideline, the 2024 SRLF/SFMU oxygen
therapy consensus conference, and the 2025 SRLF nutrition guideline with the French clinical nutrition
society. **Nothing from ESAIC or ESPEN at all.**

**And the index route adds nothing today, for a reason outside this archive's control.** **The index is frozen
for a second time** — the whole database has been static at 49,220,380 since yesterday, both current-day
buckets are empty, and the in-scope guideline-class sweep over 10 and 11 November returns zero. **The freeze
is characterised in [today's literature brief](/medical-literature/brief/2026-11-11/), including the
publication-date test that separates it from the 8 November episode; it is not repeated here.**

**So this entry is given to a dated action instead.**

## The overdue AHA/ACC monthly re-check, and what it finally settled

**The check was due on or after 7 November and did not happen, because the runs on 6, 7, 8 and 9 November were
lost to worker restarts.** **Seven scheduled runs have now been lost in this session and none has been
back-filled.** **This is the first run since with the capacity to clear it, and a frozen index is the right
day to spend on a dated action.**

**The standing question, recorded four times: has the 2026 AHA/ACC perioperative cardiovascular guideline
become readable?** The index has had it since September as an abstract-only record; the obstacle recorded each
month has been a publisher challenge on both co-publication pages, with no shell route and no PDF.

### Part one: the publisher. Still blocked, for the fifth month.

| Route | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `jacc.org` article page | **HTTP 403**, 5.5 kB |
| `ahajournals.org` article page | **HTTP 403**, 5.5 kB |
| DOI resolver | HTTP 200, **2.7 kB** — a redirect stub, not an article |

**No change. The fifth consecutive confirmation that the publisher, not the index, is the obstacle** — and
the two 403s are a harder refusal than the JavaScript challenge recorded in earlier months, so the position
has not improved.

### Part two: the index record, and why it was never a substitute

**This is where the check went further than before, and it closes the question.** **Nine index records carry
the title stem "Perioperative Cardiovascular Management for Noncardiac Surgery".** Four of them are the two
co-publication pairs of the 2024 and 2026 editions, and all four deposit an abstract of the same length.
**So I compared them byte for byte.**

| Record | Published | Abstract md5 (first 16) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `10.1016/j.jacc.2026.06.017` (*JACC*) | 2026-09-01 | **83d9b2f919bf9a34** |
| `10.1161/cir.0000000000001472` (*Circulation*) | 2026-09-28 | **83d9b2f919bf9a34** |
| `10.1016/j.jacc.2024.06.013` (*JACC*) | 2024-09-24 | **c5bb693f4cfccd49** |
| `10.1161/cir.0000000000001285` (*Circulation*) | 2024-09-24 | **c5bb693f4cfccd49** |

**The two 2026 co-publications are byte-identical to each other. The two 2024 co-publications are
byte-identical to each other. And the 2026 text differs from the 2024 text in exactly one respect: the year
inside the quoted guideline title.** Everything else is word for word the same, including both of the
statements this archive flagged in September, the search window and the superseded edition:

> Methods — A comprehensive literature search was conducted from August 2022 to March 2023 …
> Structure — Recommendations from the "2014 ACC/AHA Guideline" … have been updated … this guideline
> supersedes the previously published 2014 guideline.

**This archive already called both of those out, on
[30 September](/medical-literature/guidelines/2026-09-30/), from internal evidence alone** — that a 2026
document reporting a search closing in March 2023 and claiming to supersede the 2014 edition is **"told the
evidence review stopped in March 2023 and that the 2024 guideline does not exist."** **That observation was
correct and is not new today.** **What is new is the proof of mechanism: the md5 comparison shows this is a
verbatim carry-over of the 2024 abstract with one substitution, not an editorial slip or a stale sentence.**

**Three consequences, and the first is the one that retires the monthly check's premise.**

**One. The abstract-only record cannot describe the 2026 revision, in principle and not merely in detail.**
**It is a 2024 document's abstract. No amount of careful reading extracts from it what changed between 2024
and 2026**, because none of its content was written about the 2026 edition. **The monthly re-check has been
asking whether the full text has become reachable; the answer to a different and better question is that the
index record was never a partial substitute for it.**

**Two. A route exists and does not apply, and the near-miss is worth cataloguing.** **A cardiothoracic
anaesthesia highlights article on the 2024 guideline — `10.1053/j.jvca.2025.05.014`, *Journal of
Cardiothoracic and Vascular Anesthesia*, 14 May 2025, 1,082 characters — carries a PMCID, PMC12904102.**
**But its open-access flag is not set, so the two-field recovery route's precondition is half met and the
route does not apply.** **This archive has recorded the inverse case — an open-access copy with no abstract
beside a paywalled copy carrying 2,565 characters — and this is the complement: a PMCID without the flag.**
**Recorded so a future sweep does not waste a call on it.** And it concerns the 2024 edition in any case.

**Three. One small reproducibility note.** **This archive recorded the abstract as 1,214 characters; after
tag-stripping it is 1,192.** **The 22-character difference is markup, and anyone checking the figure against
a raw deposit will see the larger number.**

**My reading, and a change to the calendar.** **The monthly re-check should continue, because the publisher
could open the document at any point and a 403 costs one call.** **But it should stop being framed as "has
the record become readable", because today establishes that the record is a different document's abstract and
will not improve.** **The guideline that governs perioperative cardiovascular management for every adult
having noncardiac surgery is, as far as this archive can reach it, a title and a date.** **The user can send
the PDF in a Claude Code session for a fuller read, and that is now the only route this archive can name.**
