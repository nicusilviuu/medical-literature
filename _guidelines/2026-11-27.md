## No new guidelines from tracked societies today

**The pubType route returned 35 records** over `FIRST_IDATE` 18 to 27 November. **The scope filter kept 5**,
and **the dedupe log recognised all five as already reported**: the cardiac-surgery delirium consensus of
20 November, both ISHLT records, the SEDAR paediatric consensus of 25 November and the SCARTD difficult
airway guideline of 26 November.

**All 30 discards were read individually, and all 30 are genuinely out of scope** — nine French urology
guidelines in one batch again, plus oncology, gastroenterology, dermatology, neurology, nephrology,
radiology appropriateness criteria, tuberculosis, malaria, immunisation schedules, a gestational diabetes
guideline, a journal awards notice, and the two recurring borderline records already recorded as deliberate
exclusions.

**So today is a genuine empty day, and this entry exists to record that the check ran.**

## The audit that was owed

**Two misses in four days were both a journal absent from the guidance list** — the ISHLT
transplant-nursing statement on 24 November and the SCARTD airway guideline on 26 November. Each time the
journal was added afterwards. **The standing action was to stop doing it one miss at a time and audit every
tracked society against the journal it publishes guidance in.** With no new documents to report, today was
the day for it.

**The method: for each tracked society, query Europe PMC for guidance pubTypes in its journal since 2024,
confirm the journal actually carries guidance, read the exact abbreviation the index uses, and check that
abbreviation against the list** — rather than reasoning from memory about what a society's journal is
called.

**Three were missing.**

| Society | Journal, as the index abbreviates it | Guidance records since 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SPILF | `Infect Dis Now` | **17** |
| ESAIC | `Eur J Anaesthesiol` | **20** |
| SEMICYUC | `Med Intensiva (Engl Ed)` | **3** |

**The ESAIC gap is the one that matters most, and not because of the count.** `Eur J Anaesthesiol` **was
already in the brief journal list and absent from the guidance list.** So an ESAIC guideline would have been
collected for the literature brief and then **dropped by the guidance filter** — the two routes
disagreeing about the same journal. **Nothing would have surfaced that asymmetry except an audit**, because
no single day's output reveals it; the two lists are only compared when someone compares them.

**And the sample of what that journal carries makes the point concrete.** Two of the twenty are
**"Remifentanil patient-controlled analgesia for labour analgesia: guidance from..."** and
**"Intra-operative haemodynamic monitoring and management of adults having noncardiac..."** — both squarely
within this archive's scope, and one of them on a topic this archive covered from an entirely different
society six days ago.

## The exposure, measured rather than assumed

**The obvious next question is whether the gap cost anything, and the answer is no.** Each of the three
journals was queried again, restricted to records indexed **since this archive's coverage began on
17 August 2026**:

| Journal | Guidance records since 17 August 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| `Infect Dis Now` | **0** |
| `Eur J Anaesthesiol` | **0** |
| `Med Intensiva (Engl Ed)` | **0** |

**So all three holes were real, latent, and realised nothing.** The societies have published guidance into
those journals in the years before, and none since coverage started. **That is worth stating precisely
rather than letting an audit finding sound like a recovered loss** — the archive has not missed an ESAIC
guideline, it has been one guideline away from missing one for three months.

**All three journals are now in the guidance list.** Checks run on the change: **recall test 6 of 6 known
true positives on both lists**; guidance records kept today **5 before and 5 after**; brief-sweep scope
**674 before and 674 after**. **A change with zero effect on today's output, which is exactly what a
zero-exposure fix should look like.**

## Two structural findings from the same audit

**ESPEN's journal carries no guidance pubTypes at all.** `Clinical nutrition` returned **0** guidance
records since 2024 — not zero in the coverage window, zero in nearly three years. **So ESPEN guidelines are
not tagged as guidance in Europe PMC, and ESPEN output is invisible to the pubType route no matter how the
journal list is written.** That explains something this archive had not explained: why no ESPEN guideline
has ever arrived through that route. **The website route, whose working URL was found yesterday, is not a
convenience for ESPEN. It is the only route.**

**The IACTS journal is the same.** `Indian journal of thoracic and cardiovascular surgery` returned **0**
guidance records since 2024 — which sits exactly alongside yesterday's finding that the IACTS website
carries no guidance index at all. **So IACTS is invisible by both routes simultaneously**, and the honest
status for that society is not "watched" but "no readable source identified". Recorded as such.

**This is a fourth blind-spot class, and it is narrower than the three already on record.** The existing
three are: the index does not type a document as guidance; a journal series deposits no abstract; a society
publishes to its website rather than to a journal. **This one is that a society's journal exists in the
index and is never tagged as carrying guidance** — so a journal-list fix cannot help, and only the society's
own page can.

## Correction to yesterday's entry

**Yesterday's page said "third day in three that reading the discards has found something both routes
missed" and "the manual backstop has justified itself three times in three days." Both are wrong.** It was
**two finds in three days** — the ISHLT statement on 24 November and the SCARTD guideline on 26 November,
with 25 November clean.

**The error is worse than a miscount, because the sentence immediately after it said so.** The same
paragraph read: "On 24 November it was the ISHLT transplant-nursing statement... **On 25 November the
discards were clean.** Today it is a missing term." **The evidence against the claim was sitting beside the
claim.** Corrected in place on that page, with the original wording quoted.

**With today's discards clean as well, the running count is two finds in four days.**
