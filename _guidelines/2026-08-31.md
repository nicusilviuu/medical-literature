**No new guidelines from tracked societies today.**

ESC Congress 2026 closed in Munich. Everything the Congress produced on the guideline
side is already here: the
[2026 ESC heart failure guidelines and the Fifth Universal Definition of Myocardial
Infarction](/medical-literature/guidelines/2026-08-28/) on 28 August, and the
[ACVC/HFA/EAPCI cardiogenic shock diagnostic criteria](/medical-literature/guidelines/2026-08-30/)
on 30 August.

## What was checked

- **French societies** — SFAR, SRLF, SPILF: nothing in the window
- **European and international** — ESAIC, ESICM, ESPEN, SCCM, EACTS, STS, IACTS, ASA:
  nothing in the window
- **Europe PMC sweep**, 29–31 August, across Clinical Infectious Diseases, European
  Heart Journal, Anaesthesia, Intensive Care Medicine, Critical Care Medicine, Annals
  of Thoracic Surgery, British Journal of Anaesthesia, Circulation and Anesthesiology,

- **criticalcarereviews.com journal-watch**: nothing dated 31 August

That is a confirmed negative rather than an absence of looking.

## Citations resolved for the outstanding list

Two French guidelines that had been noted here without proper references are now
pinned down. Neither has an abstract deposited in Europe PMC and neither full text was
reachable, so **their content is not summarised** — they remain outstanding, but they
are now findable:

- **Intravenous administration of antibiotics by prolonged and continuous infusion** —
  Ourghanlian, d'Huart, Longuet, Boisson, Bruneel, Roger, Wolff et al.,
  *Infectious Diseases Now*, **20 December 2024**, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2024.105018,
  PMID 39710049. Joint SPILF / SFPC / SRLF / SFAR / Stabilis / RESOMEDIT work.
  A **paediatric companion** followed: *Infect Dis Now*, 27 April 2025, DOI
  10.1016/j.idnow.2025.105077.

  Worth pursuing because the [IDSA-convened multisociety sepsis position
  paper](/medical-literature/guidelines/2026-08-29/) sent on 29 August recommends
  defaulting antipseudomonal β-lactams to prolonged infusion — and the French
  societies published their version **twenty months earlier**. A side-by-side is
  the interesting piece of work here.

- Also surfaced while searching, and not previously noted: **SPILF 2026 updated
  guidelines for community-acquired urinary tract infections in adult men**
  (*Infect Dis Now*, 18 June 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2026.105312) with its
  companion rationale paper (25 June 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2026.105319), and the
  **SPILF-AEPEI position statement on antibiotic therapy and prophylaxis of infective
  endocarditis** (*Infect Dis Now*, 17 November 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2024.105011)
  — the latter directly adjacent to
  [POET-II](/medical-literature/brief/2026-08-29/).
