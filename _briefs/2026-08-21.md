## 3. FINE-HEART pooled analysis — finerenone with or without cancer history

**Journal · Published:** European Heart Journal, epublished 18 August 2026 —
secondary-scope cardiology
Ruppert et al.

Post hoc pooled analysis of FIDELIO-DKD, FIGARO-DKD and FINEARTS-HF (n=18,991);
7.3% had prior cancer. Cancer history independently predicted worse mortality and
hospitalisation, but **finerenone's benefit on all-cause death, HF events and
hospitalisation was consistent regardless of cancer history**, with the safety
profile preserved.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehag522)
