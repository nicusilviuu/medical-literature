  inhibitor-associated ketoacidosis case reports** — *Acta Anaesthesiologica Scandinavica*, 1 Jul
  2026, DOI 10.1111/aas.70254, PMID 42130079. Abstract read in full: **128 case reports from 93
  papers**; expert panel judged SAPKA **'likely' in 53 (41%), 'possible' in 38 (30%), 'unlikely' in

  cohort and the SPAQI consensus.

## New categories and gaps named 2026-10-04

- **2023 global ARDS definition trials.** TISA-818 is the first here. The definition admits non-intubated
  patients, and that trial's signal sat in its prespecified non-intubated subgroup. **Flag any paper using
  the 2023 definition on sight** — the first wave tests the definition as much as the therapy.
- **ECMO analgosedation — a cleanly identified void.** The scoping review searched December 1999 to September
  2026 across five databases, named ECMO in its objectives, and found **no studies of analgosedation
  protocols in ECMO patients.** Deep sedation, frequent paralysis, circuit drug sequestration, and the units
  most likely to have protocols. **If a paper on this appears it is a lead item.**

  or dexmedetomidine, multi-modal analgesia, or delirium assessment. Note the irony against the
  dexmedetomidine thread: **the protocols may be out of step with the guidelines, and the guidelines out of
  step with the trials.**

  (sent 10-03) cover when to operate; the BIG meta-analysis (sent 10-04) covers when not to consult.
  **Neither addresses ICP targets, osmotherapy or decompressive craniectomy timing.** That is the gap to
  fill next in this area.

## Monitoring thread — SYNTHESISED AND CLOSED as a collecting exercise

**Published as a section of the 2026-10-18 brief.** Ten documents, nine failure modes, one positive case.

**The three conditions a monitored variable must meet:**
1. **The measurement is closer to the pathology than the one it replaces.**
2. **A protocol converts the number into an action.**
3. **The reported outcome is measured by something other than the instrument.**

**Every one of the nine failures lacks at least one.** Closed-loop oxygen: 1 and 2, not 3. STOP-Vanc: 1 and 3,
not 2. AlphaMax: 2 and 3, not 1. **Commonest single failure: the third.**

**ADMISSION RULE from now on: a new document enters the thread only if it tests the three conditions. An
eleventh example does not.**

controlled more tightly AND a patient outcome improved, with nothing cancelling it.** Intensive glucose control
(14 Oct) came closest and cancelled itself. Today's disease-specific glucose paper attempts to escape the
cancellation by stratifying and its two methods disagree. **The pressure-targeting thread, seven documents, has
