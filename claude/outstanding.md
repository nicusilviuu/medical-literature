## French society guidelines never surfaced here

Citations verified 2026-08-31 via Europe PMC. None has an abstract deposited and no
full text was reachable, so all still need a proper read before being written up.
`Infect Dis Now` is the journal to watch for SPILF/SFAR/SRLF output — searching
`AUTH:"SPILF" OR TITLE:"SPILF"` in Europe PMC lists their recent guidelines well.

- **Intravenous administration of antibiotics by prolonged and continuous infusion**
  — Ourghanlian, d'Huart, Longuet, Boisson, Bruneel, Roger, Wolff et al., *Infect Dis
  Now*, 2024-12-20, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2024.105018, PMID 39710049. Joint
  SPILF/SFPC/SRLF/SFAR/Stabilis/RESOMEDIT. Paediatric companion: *Infect Dis Now*,
  2025-04-27, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2025.105077.
  **Highest value of these** — the IDSA multisociety sepsis position paper (sent
  2026-08-29) recommends default prolonged-infusion antipseudomonal β-lactams; the
  French societies got there 20 months earlier. Do the side-by-side.
- **SPILF-AEPEI position statement: antibiotic therapy and prophylaxis of infective
  endocarditis** — *Infect Dis Now*, 2024-11-17, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2024.105011.
  Directly adjacent to POET-II (sent 2026-08-29).
- **SPILF 2026 guidelines: community-acquired urinary tract infections in adult men**
  — *Infect Dis Now*, 2026-06-18, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2026.105312, with rationale
  paper 2026-06-25, DOI 10.1016/j.idnow.2026.105319.
- **SPILF/SPLF update on community-acquired pneumonia in adults**, with SRLF, SFM,
  SFR, SFMU. Citation still unverified.
